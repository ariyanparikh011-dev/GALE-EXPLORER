# GALE-EXPLORER
A 3D exploration and visualization project of Gale Crater on Mars using NASA datasets.

## About the Project

GALE EXPLORER is a 3D Mars exploration project focused on Gale Crater, the region being explored by NASA's Curiosity rover.

Our goal is to bring NASA data together in one place to help people better understand the Martian landscape and the challenges future astronauts may face when exploring the planet.

## Features

- **3D Mars Terrain:** A three-dimensional model of Gale Crater created using elevation data.
- **Curiosity Rover Route:** Displays the actual route traveled by NASA's Curiosity rover.
- **First-Person Exploration:** Allows users to move around and explore the Martian landscape.
- **Martian Environment:** Includes terrain textures, rocks, and a Mars-inspired sky.

## NASA Data

Our project uses NASA datasets, including:

- Gale Crater elevation data from the Mars Express High Resolution Stereo Camera (HRSC).
- Curiosity rover traverse data from NASA's Mars mapping resources.

## Use of AI

We used AI tools throughout development to help us learn unfamiliar software, understand NASA datasets, solve coding problems, and bring our ideas to life. AI helped guide our development, but our team made the decisions about the project's design and direction.

## Tools Used

- Unity 6
- QGIS

## Our Goal

We hope GALE EXPLORER can help people better understand Mars and support future ideas for astronaut exploration, route planning, and scientific research.

This project is an early prototype, and we hope to continue improving it by adding more NASA datasets and exploration features.
